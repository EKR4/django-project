About Our Club

A Legacy of Excellence Since 1965

Nestled in the rolling hills of the countryside, Pinecrest Golf Club has been a premier destination for golf enthusiasts for over five decades. Our 18-hole championship course, designed by renowned architect Robert Trent Jones Jr., offers a challenging yet enjoyable experience for players of all skill levels.

Beyond the course, our club features exceptional practice facilities, a state-of-the-art clubhouse, and dining options that rival the finest restaurants. We pride ourselves on creating a welcoming atmosphere where members can enjoy the game they love in a beautiful setting.
